app-build-playbook · v1.1.0

From idea to tested build, one gate at a time

Eight stages. Each one ends at a gate you can check. Reviewers advise and you decide. Whenever a decision is needed, you get grilled: rounds of questions, each with a recommended answer you can accept with “yes”. Tap a stage to see what happens inside it and which lessons shaped it.

Reading a finding ID

Rstage-SAreviewer3round-02number

Stage: R design docs · A adopted code · P1–Pn build. The panel is round 0. Examples: R-PP0-04 (pathologist, panel), P2-CR1-06.

Every audit opens with

Critical: 0 · Major: 0 · Minor: 4 · Nit: 2
CriticalCould cause harm or wrong advice, or acts without your Confirm.
MajorMaterially hurts correctness or real-life use.
MinorPolish. Batched, never looped.

How a grill round looks

❓ Q1 - Winter watering: pause, or stretch the interval?
➡️ Stretch to every 14 days.

Answer “yes to all”, or “yes except Q2: (b)”. Questions that depend on your answers come in the next round. Facts get looked up, never asked. Adapted from Matt Pocock’s grilling skill.
